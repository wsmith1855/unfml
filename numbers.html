The numbers

Who is on the list, what a 3.5% raise costs, and what that money could fund.

Who is on the list

Every executive, dean, chair and faculty member at Michigan State with reported 2025 pay. Those paid $200,000 or more are named on the salary list. Lower pay bands are counted but not named. Chairs and deans are shown separately because they hold both faculty and administrative roles.

What the raise costs, and what it could fund

Every one of the people that were laid off due to budget cuts over the last year could have kept their jobs.

The raise starts at 3.5%. Change it, or enter the salary of a typical employee in your unit, and the totals update as you type.
