How it was compiled

Where the numbers come from, how they were put together, and where they fall short.

Sources and method

  • Source data. The Mackinac Center for Public Policy's Michigan Government Salaries Database, built from public records requests. The data reflects 2025 pay.
  • Reported pay, not base salary. The database lists total gross pay, which can include coaching, summer pay, overtime, and leave payouts. Salary can differ from base pay, and the totals here are approximate for that reason.
  • Who is included. Michigan State employees with 2025 reported pay whose job title includes Professor (including associate, assistant, distinguished and endowed-chair professors), Chairperson, Dean (including associate, assistant and senior associate deans), or Executive Management (vice presidents, provosts, assistant and associate vice presidents, executive directors, and similar). The President is included. Coaches are not, apart from the Athletic Director, whose title is Vice President. Professional-level staff titles that merely contain the word Professional are not counted as faculty. Everyone with reported pay is counted, and only those paid $200,000 or more are named. Pay between $100,000 and $199,999 and under $100,000 appears as counts and combined totals.
  • Lower-paid faculty. The under-$100,000 group includes part-time, fixed-term and partial-year appointments, so reported pay can be far below a full-time salary, and not everyone in it necessarily received the 3.5% raise. The database is also updated on a rolling basis, and some roles use titles that do not match these groups, so the counts may be slightly off.
  • Support staff comparison. The headcount of about 6,200 comes from MSU HR's support staff handbook. The default average salary of $75,000 is an estimate from the same salary database: the average 2025 pay of about 6,900 employees in support staff job titles (clerical, service, trades, police, IT, professional and administrative titles, including supervisory titles marked /S), leaving out faculty, academic staff, postdoctoral scholars, instructors, coaches, executives and anyone paid under $35,000, which removes most part-time and partial-year pay. The median is about $70,000, and clerical, service and trades titles alone average about $54,000. Because job titles do not show union membership, the group is an approximation, and the database counts more people than the HR headcount because it includes anyone paid at any point in 2025. You can change the average, because total support staff payroll is not published in the sources used here. The comparison shows the scale of the money, not an audited figure.
  • Raise percentages. Support staff received 1.9%. Executives and faculty received 3.5%, which is 1.6 percentage points more. The calculator lets you change the percentage.
  • Data pulled. Salary records were collected in November 2026 from the Mackinac Center database at mackinac.org/salaries. The database updates on a rolling basis, so later pulls may differ.
  • Comments. Comments are reviewed by a person before anything is posted. They are opinions from visitors and are not verified by this site.
  • Found a mistake? Use the comment form and say what is wrong. Corrections are reviewed the same way as comments.
