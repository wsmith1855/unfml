Bars drawn to scale.

Through COVID, support staff were declared essential. Today, an executive return-to-campus mandate puts them back on site two to three days a week, and the bill lands on them: parking, gas, and East Lansing’s city income tax, along with the work/life balance many had proven to be more than effective with.

Counting the 3,216 executives, deans, chairs and faculty with reported 2025 pay, 3.5% is approximately $16.6 million. The 1.6-point gap alone is $7.6 million.

Dollar figures apply 3.5% to everyone on the list with reported 2025 pay, using public records. Pay is gross, and some lower-paid faculty are part-time or partial-year, so totals are approximate. See how the list was compiled.
